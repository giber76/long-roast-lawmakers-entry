# The Long Roast: Lawmakers and the File They Cannot Convert

Entry for [The Long Roast: Lawmakers and the File They Cannot Convert](https://make.fun/bounty/u-swift-raven-16-the-long-roast-lawmakers-and-the-f3a3bdf7)
on make.fun — one satirical sentence, at most 320 characters, roasting
lawmakers who would regulate AI, crypto or the internet but cannot convert
a file to PDF.

## The roast (194 characters)

> They will decide what your crypto wallet may do, what your AI may say and what your browser may show, but the bill itself is a .docx their office has been trying to convert to PDF since Tuesday.

## Why this sentence

- **Authority first, then the undercut:** it opens on the full sweep of
  their power — your wallet, your AI, your browser — so the setup is
  authority, not a generic "old people don't get tech" gag.
- **Specific, unexpected detail:** the bill itself is a `.docx` their
  office has been trying to convert to PDF *since Tuesday* — the
  conversion has been failing for days while the legislating races ahead.
- **Aim on the lawmakers and the rules they write:** the joke is about
  power and competence, not random abuse.
- **Punch on the last words:** "since Tuesday" lands the joke and stays
  quotable in one pass.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the sentence to
`/output/entry.txt`. Run locally with:

```
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
